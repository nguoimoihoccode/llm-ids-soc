# DDoS Playbook

DDoS activity usually involves high traffic volume, high packet rate, or many similar requests targeting a service.

Recommended response:

- Confirm whether traffic volume exceeds normal baseline.
- Apply rate limiting, WAF/CDN protections, or upstream filtering.
- Check target service health and latency.
- Preserve flow evidence for incident review.
- Map to MITRE ATT&CK technique T1498 where appropriate.
